This Privacy Policy explains how Barrier Duel ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app. Barrier Duel is a strategy board game (a Quoridor-style game) for Android. The App's core gameplay works entirely offline and does not require an account, sign-in, or internet connection to play.

Summary

  • We do not operate our own servers and do not collect or store your data ourselves.
  • All game data (player nicknames, coin balance, settings, playtime) is stored only on your device.
  • The App shows ads via Google AdMob, which may collect data as described below.
  • The App includes an age screen and applies stricter, non-personalized, child-safe ad settings for players who indicate they are children.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described here.

1. Information Stored Locally On Your Device

The App uses Android's local storage (DataStore) to save your preferences and progress. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and is deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

2. Information Collected by Advertising Partners

The App displays banner and interstitial ads using Google AdMob. To show ads, Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device information, and general location, and may set identifiers used for ad delivery and (where permitted) ad personalization. This processing is carried out by Google, not by us — we do not receive or store this data ourselves.

The App requests the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions solely so ads can be requested and loaded; these permissions are not used for any other purpose, and all gameplay logic runs fully offline.

  • How Google uses information from partner sites or apps

3. Consent for Ads (GDPR / UK / US State Privacy Laws)

For players who indicate they are not children, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check whether consent or a disclosure is legally required in your region (for example under GDPR in the EEA/UK, or applicable U.S. state privacy laws) and, if so, shows you Google's consent form before requesting personalized ads. In most regions no form is required and this happens invisibly. You can change your consent choice at any time from the App's Settings screen (where a "Privacy / Ad Choices" option is provided) or by clearing app data, which resets the consent flow.

4. Children's Privacy & the Age Screen

Barrier Duel is an all-ages game, and on first launch it asks whether the current player is a child. This choice is not sent to us or stored anywhere except on your own device.

  • If "child" is selected: the App tells Google's ad SDK to treat all ad requests as child-directed and made under the age of consent. Ads are always non-personalized and capped to "G" (general audience) content — no ad personalization consent is ever requested from a child, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.
  • If "standard" is selected: ads are capped to "PG" content, and the consent flow described in Section 3 applies.

We do not knowingly collect personal information (such as names, contact details, or precise location) from any player, child or adult — the only "personal" data in the App (an optional nickname) is stored solely on-device and never leaves it.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal information, because we do not collect or hold any ourselves. The only third party involved is Google (AdMob / UMP) for ad delivery and consent management, as described above.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Locally stored data (nickname, coins, settings, playtime, age-screen choice) persists on your device until you clear the App's storage or uninstall it, at which point it is permanently deleted. Since we hold no copy of this data on any server, there is nothing further for us to delete on request. Data that Google's ad SDKs may have collected is governed by Google's own retention practices — see the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

7. Your Choices

  • Reset ad consent: use the Settings screen's privacy option, or clear the App's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Barrier Duel → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Limit ad tracking: use your device's ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization" / "Delete advertising ID").
  • Delete all local data: uninstall the App, or clear its storage as above.

8. Children Under 13 (or applicable age in your region)

If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information to us directly (outside the age-gated, non-personalized flow described above), please contact us using the details below so we can address it — though as noted, we do not operate servers that receive such information.
